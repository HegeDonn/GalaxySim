# Contribution instructions

Do not add automated assistant or tool-provider attribution, promotional credits,
co-author trailers, session links, or generated-by signatures to project files,
commit messages, pull requests, or release notes. Describe the actual changes
and their verification. Preserve required third-party licenses and legitimate
human contributor attribution.

Keep personal names, email addresses, account handles and absolute local checkout
paths out of public files. Follow PUBLICATION.md when preparing a release; the
original development history is private and must not be pushed as public history.
